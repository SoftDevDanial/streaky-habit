# Security audit, app

> Written by the security finder (read-only), 2026-10-09, against `6cfe05f` on `main`. Scope: `index.html`, `src/storage.js`, `src/view.js`, `src/app.js`, `src/styles.css`, against the running app (served with `python -m http.server <port> --bind 127.0.0.1`, headless Playwright Chromium 149). Judged against the Threat model (T1 to T12) and R21 to R26 in `spec.md`, `docs/constitution.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. The closed `habit-logic` module is treated as its contract. Line numbers are as of `6cfe05f`. Evidence: `docs/modules/app/qa/security/20261009T165000/` (`evidence.md` indexes every probe and its output).

## Verdict

**FAIL (issues_found). 0 CRITICAL, 1 HIGH, 0 MEDIUM, 4 LOW.**

Eleven of the twelve threat-model rows held under hostile input in the running app. The one that did not is T7 (accidental deletion): a double click or double tap on a habit's trash button can press "Delete habit" in the dialog it opens, because the two buttons overlap on screen (SEC-01). The habit and its whole history are deleted and saved with no deliberate second action. Nothing in the threat model covers this path.

No script injection, prototype pollution, selector injection, network egress or crash was found. The baseline passes: `python -m pytest tests/browser -q` gave 127 passed, `node --test "tests/**/*.test.js"` gave 99 pass, 0 fail.

## HIGH

### SEC-01: A double click or double tap on the trash button deletes the habit through the dialog it opens (T7 bypass)
- **File:** `src/styles.css:454-468` (`.dialog`, fixed and centred, with no pointer lock during its 200 ms fade-in), `src/styles.css:488-493` (`.dialog-actions`, Delete habit in the right column), `src/styles.css:162` (`.habit-head .icon-btn`, the trash button pulled to the page edge), `src/app.js:43-46` (`askToDelete`), `src/app.js:61-67` (`onDialogClose`, which deletes on `returnValue === "delete"`) · **Boundary:** a pointer gesture on the trash button → `showModal()` → the second pointer event of the same gesture → "Delete habit" → `deleteHabit` → `saveHabits`
- **Threat:** The dialog opens under the finger. It is hit-testable from its first frame (measured: 4 ms after the click the element under the pointer is the dialog while its opacity is still 0, `probe_05c`), and "Delete habit" sits where the trash button was on common screens. A second click or tap at the same point is delivered to "Delete habit", which submits the dialog form with `returnValue "delete"`. The habit and its whole history go, the state is saved, and there is no undo (a spec non-goal). This is the accident T7 exists to prevent. T7's listed controls (focus on Cancel, Esc, backdrop click) all hold (see "What held up"), but none of them is involved: the gesture never reaches the keyboard or the backdrop.
  - **Geometry.** On viewports up to about 450 px wide the right edge of "Delete habit" (viewport width minus 44) lies 8 px right of the trash button's left edge (width minus 52), so the left 8 px column of the trash button is also "Delete habit" (28 px at 320 wide, 18 px at 330, 8 px from 340 to 440, 3 px at 450). From 1000 px up the dialog's right column covers 14 to 34 px of the trash button (`probe_05b`, 12 habits, the page scrolled so that the row sits at the dialog's height). Widths 460 to 900 had no overlap (460 to 550 step 10, 600, 700, 800, 899, 900).
  - **No scrolling needed.** With the page at the top, the third habit's trash button overlaps "Delete habit" at 390x844 (8 x 27 px), 412x915 (8 x 30 px) and 1440x900 (34 x 23 px). It does not at 1280x800, 1366x768, 1920x1080 or 375x667 (`probe_05e`, 1 to 12 habits, one-line and two-line titles). With a list long enough to scroll, any row can be put at the dialog's height, and every height tested (568 to 915) had an overlap.
- **Reproduction (deterministic, run from the project root):**
  1. `python docs/modules/app/qa/security/20261009T165000/probe_05f_repro_sample.py` serves the app itself, seeds the three-habit sample from `docs/design/sample-history.json` and fixes the clock at 2026-10-08 12:00 +08:00.
  2. Desktop, 1440x900: the third habit's trash box is (716,501)-(760,545) and "Delete habit" is (726,476)-(896,524). A single click at (743,512) only opens the dialog (the control). A double click at the same point leaves `['Read 20 pages', 'Stretch 10 minutes']`, and `localStorage` holds the two-habit state. The point is on the upper half of the trash icon.
  3. Phone, 412x915, touch: overlap (360,484)-(368,513). A double tap at (364,498), 18 px left of the button centre, deletes the habit.
  4. `probe_05g_gap_sweep.py`: at 1440x900 with a mouse and at 412x915 with touch, two activations at that point delete the habit at every gap from 0 to 700 ms. At 390x844 the overlap is the 8 x 27 px column. A mouse double click deleted there (`probe_05b`, part 3). With touch the result varied: deleted with a 60 ms gap in `probe_05b` and at 0 ms in `probe_05g`, kept for gaps of 40 ms and more in `probe_05g` and with a 100 ms gap in `probe_05f`. Treat that width as borderline, not safe.
  5. `probe_05d_click_detail.py`: the second click reaches "Delete habit" with `event.detail` 2 and 166 to 189 ms after the first, for mouse and touch.
- **Maps to spec threat:** T7 (its mitigations are bypassed by this vector). NEW as a vector, not in the threat model.
- **Fix:** Arm "Delete habit" only after a short window, in `app.js`, with no timer and no clock read. Record the opening click's `event.timeStamp` where the dialog is opened (`onListClick` or `askToDelete`), and add a capture-phase `click` listener on the dialog that calls `preventDefault()` for a click on `button.btn-danger` when `event.timeStamp - openedAt` is under about 500 ms (`preventDefault` on a submit button's click cancels the form submission, so the dialog stays open). This is a JS change, because R2 locks `src/styles.css` to the golden rules. A weaker option is to ignore a Delete click with `event.detail > 1`, which stops a double click or tap but not a slow second click.
  - **Verified in memory** (`probe_10_fix_sketches.py`, route interception, `src/` not touched, 500 ms window, suite-style fixed clock): two activations 0, 120 and 300 ms apart at the overlap keep the habit at 1440x900 (mouse) and 412x915 (touch). A deliberate click after the window deletes, Enter on the focused Delete button after the window deletes, Cancel works at once, 0 page errors.
  - **Tests and spec for the solver:** the tests that click "Delete habit" straight after opening (`open_delete_dialog` in `conftest.py`, `test_delete.py`, `live_e2e.py`) need to wait out the window. Add a regression test that double clicks, and double taps with touch emulation, the computed overlap point on the sample at 1440x900 and 412x915 and expects the habit kept (it must fail against `6cfe05f`, as `probe_05f` does). The orchestrator should add a T7 note for this vector and a line in R13 or R14 for the arming window.

### SEC-02: A bidirectional override in a name garbles the confirmation dialog's own words, and combining marks stack past the row
- **File:** `src/view.js:301` (`#del-title` text is `Delete "${habitName}"?`), `src/view.js:94` (`.habit-name`) · **Boundary:** stored or typed name → `textContent` of `h2#del-title`
- **Threat:** The list is safe, because the UA style sheet gives `h3` and `h2` `unicode-bidi: isolate` (measured: `ltr/isolate` on `h3.habit-name`), so an override cannot leave its heading, and heatmap labels contain no names. But the dialog title puts the name between two literal quotes in the same text node, so an unterminated override reorders the dialog's own punctuation. A name `U+202E Reversed text here` shows `Delete "?"ereh txet desreveR`, and `Read 20 pages U+202E dleiys .gnp` shows `Delete "Read 20 pagessyield ?"png.` (`shots/dlg-rlo-prefix.png`, `shots/dlg-rlo-spoof-tail.png`). In the list that second name reads `Read 20 pagespng. syield`. A name of one base letter plus 39 combining marks overprints the brand and the dialog title (`shots/dlg-zalgo.png`). A name of 40 zero-width spaces gives a blank heading. Nothing was exploitable: the habit named in the title is still the habit deleted, the page never scrolled horizontally at 320, 360 or 390 px for any of 20 name shapes, and only the typist or a sibling page on the origin can plant such a name. It is a display spoof of the one screen meant to prevent a wrong delete.
- **Maps to spec threat:** NEW, not in the threat model. It touches T3 and T7. The `habit-logic` audit left the name characters as a product decision (observation).
- **Fix:** Isolate the name in the title with FSI and PDI (`Delete "\u2068${habitName}\u2069"?`). It keeps one text node, so R24's "no child elements" still holds, and it contains any number of unterminated overrides. Verified in memory (`probe_10`, `shots/fix-rlo-prefix.png`: the closing quote and question mark stay at the end). The title text then differs from `Delete "<name>"?` by two invisible characters, so R13 and its test need a dated note. The alternative is to reject bidi format characters (U+202A to U+202E, U+2066 to U+2069, U+200E, U+200F, U+061C) in `validateName`, which is a `habit-logic` change and a product decision.

### SEC-03: T9 covers a sibling page writing the key, not reading it
- **File:** `docs/modules/app/spec.md` T9 and the Assets line, `src/storage.js:11` (`STORAGE_KEY`) · **Boundary:** any script on any page of `<user>.github.io` → `localStorage["streaks.state"]`
- **Threat:** The Invariants say other pages on the origin "can read and write" the key, and T9 mitigates only the write side (a foreign value is unreadable and is reset). The read side has no control: a sibling page, or a third-party script that a sibling page loads, reads every habit name and the whole history. `probe_07` showed a sibling page reading the stored text. `script-src 'self'` does not help, because `'self'` is the whole origin, so any script file anywhere under `<user>.github.io` passes it. The Assets line names privacy ("No alcohol") and "nothing leaves the device" as the top assets. Precondition: a hostile or compromised script on another page of the same Pages origin, visited by the same browser.
- **Maps to spec threat:** T9 (incomplete). The residual is acknowledged in the Invariants but not carried into T9, the README or the promise.
- **Fix:** No code change. Serve the app from an origin that hosts nothing else (a custom domain, or the repository that is the user site itself) and say so in the README, or add the read side to T9's accepted residual and to the user-facing claim.

### SEC-04: The publish scope is undefined, and "serves the folder as is" would publish the whole repository
- **File:** `CLAUDE.md` Stack ("GitHub Pages, which serves the folder as is"), spec R26 · **Boundary:** the static host serving the files (trust boundary 4) → the public web
- **Threat:** There is no deploy configuration: no workflow, no `.nojekyll`, no CNAME, no git remote. Pointing Pages at the repository root would publish all 639 tracked files: `docs/` (611 files, about 9 MB, including QA screenshots and process ledgers), `tests/`, `CLAUDE.md`, and the deliberate mutant copies of `src/` that earlier audits kept under `docs/modules/habit-logic/qa/**/mirrors/` (they call `fetch` and `sessionStorage` on purpose). About 33 files hold local absolute paths (`{workspace}/...`) and 2 hold the Windows user name. No credential-shaped string was found (a pattern scan of every tracked file, plus the project's own gate). R26 says `docs/design/sample-history.json` is "tests only, never shipped", which a deploy of the folder as is would break. None of this runs in the app, so the impact is disclosure of build records and local paths.
- **Maps to spec threat:** NEW. Trust boundary 4 is listed but no row covers what is served.
- **Fix:** Publish only `index.html` and `src/` (a deploy workflow that uploads those two paths, or a `gh-pages` branch that holds only them), add `.nojekyll`, and state the publish scope in `CLAUDE.md`.

### SEC-05: With the device clock at year 10000 or later, the app saves a day key its own reader rejects, and the next load wipes every habit
- **File:** `src/app.js:14-16` (`readToday`), `src/storage.js:27` (`isDateKey` over `done`), `src/storage.js:84-85` (the reset) · **Boundary:** device clock → `localDateKey` → `saveHabits` → `decodeState` → reset
- **Threat:** `localDateKey` returns `10000-01-01` for a local year of 10000, which fails `isDateKey` (four digits). With the clock set there (`probe_08`: Playwright clock at 9999-12-31 23:59:30 UTC, local date 10000-01-01), adding a habit and ticking today saved `{"version":1,"habits":[{"name":"Clock test","done":["10000-01-01"]}]}`, and the reload showed the reset note with no habits. The app writes a state that its decoder calls unreadable, then destroys it. Years 0001, 1970 and 2100 behaved. The precondition is a device clock in year 10000 or later (JavaScript dates reach year 275760), so this is not reachable remotely and not realistic. The `habit-logic` audit recorded the same edge as an observation.
- **Maps to spec threat:** NEW, a variant of T1 (a reader and a writer that disagree).
- **Fix:** Accept it and write it down, or make `readToday()` refuse a key that fails `isDateKey` (do not save while it does), or let `decodeState` read what the writer can write. A note in the spec is enough.

## What GitHub Pages changes, and whether the accepted residuals hold

- **Headers.** Pages sends none of the app's choosing (the spec's own premise; no network was used here, so Pages' actual headers were not measured). The meta policy is the only CSP. It is in force for everything after it (it precedes `link` and `script`), and it enforced `script-src`, `connect-src`, `form-action`, `base-uri`, `object-src` and `default-src` in the probes. It cannot carry `frame-ancestors`, `report-uri` or `sandbox`. Nosniff, a referrer policy, a permissions policy and X-Frame-Options are not available either, and the app does not depend on any of them.
- **Shared origin.** Every project page of the user shares one origin. That is the T9 and SEC-03 exposure: writes are handled, reads are not, and `script-src 'self'` covers the whole origin.
- **Publish scope.** "Serves the folder as is" is SEC-04.
- **Residuals.** T6 holds (silent loss of unsaved changes on reload, verified). A sibling page that fills the quota can trigger it on purpose, which is inside the same accepted residual. T8 holds with the figures above. T9 holds for writes. T10 holds in a browser that partitions third-party storage, with the two qualifiers. T12 holds (last save wins, verified).
- **Cache.** Pages caches for minutes, so a fix such as SEC-01 reaches users after that delay. No security effect beyond lag.

## Observations (not filed)

- **Spellcheck.** The name input does not set `spellcheck="false"`. In browsers where the user has turned on a cloud spellchecker, typed habit names (for example "No alcohol") can leave the device through the browser, outside the app's control. The goldens are immutable, so this is a design decision.
- **Phosphor notice.** The head comment credits Phosphor Icons (MIT). The MIT terms ask that the copyright notice accompany copies. `docs/compliance/` is empty. This belongs to the compliance gate.
- **Invisible names.** A name of only zero-width characters is valid (the `habit-logic` audit noted it).
- **No JavaScript-independent guard on the add form.** The form has no `method` or `action`, so without the meta CSP a script-less submit would put the name in the URL (the CSP-free control in `probe_03e` shows it). The CSP is the stated mitigation and it holds.
- **Tooling.** Playwright waits that poll inside the page stall once a fake clock is installed. These probes use `expect()` after `clock.install`.
