Reading. A today key in October to December of the years 10000 to 10009 makes `yearMonths` walk back month by month
past year 1 without ever meeting the month of its `yearStart` ("0999d-1", a prefix `formatKey` never writes), so the
call never returns and its months array grows until the heap cap (256 MB reached in about 3.3 s). January to
September of any five digit year, and six digit years, throw a TypeError at once (month 0). Later five digit
Octobers to Decembers return 11 one-day months whose keys fail `isDateKey`, and `toggleDay` accepts none of them.
Done today (the today key itself) is refused at every far clock. The year 1000 key of habit-logic O1 is accepted by
`toggleDay` but no control offers it.
