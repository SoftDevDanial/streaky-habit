tick check that waited for "true" only) ran into the shell's 590 s timeout with no output. Its tick checks could never
be met when the day was already done, so each waited out its cap. The process list right after showed none of its
processes alive (no chrome.exe, no http.server of mine, only another agent's pytest WebKit run, pid 23720, started
13:29, parent 3784, left alone). The probe was then split and fixed (flip check, unbuffered output) as on disk.
The quota is the same 5,242,880 UTF-16 units (5 MiB) in both engines. A 5.1 M character value is the worst case.
Desktop numbers only. Another agent's WebKit test run was on the machine during these runs, so times may be high.
