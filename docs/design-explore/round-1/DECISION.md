# Round 1 decision: Streaks

**Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Decided by:** orchestrator under the owner's delegation (**ORCH**, reversible)
**Compare page:** `compare.html` in this folder.

## Picked
**B, Quiet beads.** The streak is drawn, not only counted. Done days are joined by a thread, and a dashed
thread runs from yesterday into today's open bead, which shows the brainstorm rule "a streak does not
break until today is over" with no extra copy.

## Changes to B before locking the system (no round 2)
1. **Heatmap levels 1 and 2 must differ more**, in dot size and in tone, so the five levels read apart
   without relying on colour.
2. **Keep** the labels B added to the heatmap: the heading "Last 12 weeks", the legend ends "None" and
   "All", and the month and weekday axis labels. They name parts of capability C5 and add no feature.
3. **Use one sample history everywhere**: `../final/sample-history.json`, generated so that every fixed
   value in the brief holds (week strips, current and best streaks, the last 7 heatmap levels).

## Rejected
- **A, Wall calendar.** A strong, familiar idea, but a cross half reads as wrong and the boxed grid is busy.
- **C, Scoreboard.** The big number suits a sprint more than a calm routine, and the tall tiles push the
  add field and the heatmap below the first screen.
