T3The orbit S

Two days in a row write the S, each day ring keeping one thick arc with its ends cut square.

Construction

Two rings, centre line radius 6, band 4, touching at the middle, stacked like two days in one column of the band. Each keeps 250° of arc, cut square on its own radius, so the slit where they meet is 4 units, as wide as the band. At 28 px the band is 3.5 px, the weight of a done arc in the 12 week band. The 16 px tile halves it on whole pixels.
