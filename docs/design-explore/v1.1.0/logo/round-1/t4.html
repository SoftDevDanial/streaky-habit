T4Wound up

One thick arc spirals a lap inward into the filled core of a full day, the streak winding tighter.

Construction

Two half turns of one 4 unit band. The right half circles C1, the core's centre (radii 10 to 14). The left half steps in half a pitch to C2, 4 lower (dashed, radii 6 to 10), and lands in the radius 6 core. Gap 4 for half a lap, then it closes. Cut square at 12 o'clock, clockwise like the app.
