Construction

Grid. 28 units, 1 unit per pixel of the 28 px header. The 1.0 build in proportion, beads 9, thread 5, gaps 4, open ring 2 round a 5 hole. Ring weight. The arc is concentric with the corner bead, gap 2, band 3, from 9 to 12 o'clock, ends cut square half a unit past the bead's axes like the app's segment ends, so they land on whole pixels. Band 3, thicker than the open ring's 2. Balance. The 1.0 sat 2 right and 1 down of 112 because its run outweighed the open bead. The arc fills the empty top left and brings the ink's centre back to the middle, so the drawing now sits half a unit up and left, a hair above centre.

Below 28 px use the 16 grid master, shown at 18 px and in both tiles. The 16 px trade.
