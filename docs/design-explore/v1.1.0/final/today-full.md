# S3 Twelve habits

**Golden** `today-full.html`
**Inventory** S3, the 12-habit limit (C1)

## Intent
Show that the list frame scrolls while the page and the field stay put, and that the field says why it is closed.

## What the golden shows
Twelve rows from `sample-history.json`, the frame scrolled to the top with the bottom fade on (`.frame-wrap.more-below`). Each band ring has twelve 21 degree segments, which reads as a dotted circle (f2 notes this). The composer is `.is-full`, the label and note in `--ink-soft`, the field and Add disabled. The field sits exactly where it sits in K1.
