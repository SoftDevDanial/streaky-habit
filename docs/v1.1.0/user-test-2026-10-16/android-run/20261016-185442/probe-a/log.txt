Arithmetic, from the CSS the app and the golden share (src/styles.css and the golden's own style, min-width 768 rules).
All habits uses .months.months-all { grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fill, 294px); column gap 20 px }, so three
months need 3 x 294 + 2 x 20 = 922 px. The dialog is min(980 px, 100vw - 48 px), its body has 24 px padding each side.
At 1280 and wider: 980 - 2 border - 48 padding = 930 px of room with no scrollbar (three fit), 920 px with the
10 px scrollbar drawn (two fit). At 1024: 926 px or 916 px. Three months fit only when the scrollbar takes 8 px or
less (4 px or less at 1024). The single-habit year uses .months { repeat(3, max-content) } at 1024 and wider, a fixed
three columns, so its top row holds three months whether a scrollbar is drawn or not.

What the design promises. docs/design/overview-year.md: "Desktop, a centred 980 px dialog over K1 under --scrim,
three months across in 42 px columns, 40 px rings in 44 px cells, numbers 16 px." The CSS comment in both the golden
and the app reads "P4: the overview's 40 px ring in a 42 px column keeps the 16 px day number and fits three months
across". The golden's own markup, rendered with a drawn scrollbar, shows two months across, the same as the app.
